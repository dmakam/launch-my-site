# Industry starters

For common small businesses, most of the site structure is predictable. Use the starter to plan pages and sections, then **ask only about the gaps**. Everything in the "Ask" column is a question for the owner. Never fill it in yourself.

If the business isn't listed, use the closest one or skip this file.

## Home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, cleaning)

Visitors are often in a hurry (something broke) and deciding who to trust.

- **Pages:** Home, Services, About, Service Areas, Book / Contact.
- **Must-have sections:** phone number in a sticky bar or header on every page; "how a visit works" (call → quote → fix); service areas list; booking or quote form.
- **Trust section:** licence number, insured, years in business, warranties or guarantees, real reviews. Show only what they confirm.
- **Ask:**
  - Which services do you offer? (list typical ones for them to tick)
  - Homes only, or businesses too?
  - Do you offer 24/7 or emergency service?
  - Which cities or areas do you cover?
  - Licence number, insurance, years in business, warranties?
  - Any financing or free estimates?
  - A few real customer reviews, or a Google profile link?

## Restaurants, cafes, bakeries

- **Pages:** Home, Menu, About, Visit Us (hours, map, parking), Order / Reserve.
- **Must-have sections:** hours and address above the fold on phone; menu as real text, not a PDF; order or reservation button.
- **Ask:** online ordering or reservation link? Dietary labels? Catering or private events? Photos of the food and space?

## Salons, spas, barbers, fitness

- **Pages:** Home, Services & Prices, Team, Book.
- **Must-have sections:** booking button everywhere; price list; team with photos (theirs only).
- **Ask:** booking system link? Prices to show, or "from" prices? Team names and photos with permission? Gift cards?

## Professional services (law, accounting, consulting, real estate)

- **Pages:** Home, Services / Practice Areas, About / Team, Contact.
- **Must-have sections:** who you help and with what; credentials; a clear "book a consultation" path.
- **Ask:** areas of practice? Credentials, bar or licence numbers they want shown? Required disclaimers? Consultation free or paid?

## Contractors and builders (remodeling, landscaping, construction)

- **Pages:** Home, Services, Projects (gallery), About, Get a Quote.
- **Must-have sections:** project photos (theirs only); quote form with project type and budget range; licence and insurance.
- **Ask:** project photos they own? Typical project size? Licence and insurance details? Areas served?

## How to use the "Ask" list

- Turn it into one message with tickable options where possible: "Which of these do you offer? AC repair, new systems, heating repair, tune-ups, duct work, 24/7 emergency."
- If they skip something, leave that section out of the build. Mention it once in the plan: "I left out reviews until you send some."
